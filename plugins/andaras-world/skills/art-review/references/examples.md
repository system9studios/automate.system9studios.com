# Examples

**Prompt:** "Review CA-001 A–D."
**Behavior:** Loads art-review, reads the CA-001 brief, views all four images. C shows a neighboring city's insignia on the checkpoint: implied fact "the city garrisons the gate" → `[PROPOSED]`, and a possible contradiction if the village is independent. B's silhouette reads as a medieval castle gate: a tone flag, naming the guardrail it breaks. Recommends A with an iterate delta ("add patched repairs at head height; keep the overpass read"). One next action: the owner selects.

**Prompt:** "Here's my Krita sketch of the inn. Does it fit the world?"
**Behavior:** Reviews the design against canon (materials, era, tone). Doesn't critique line quality or technique unless asked, and offers creative-coach for that.

**Non-trigger:** "Generate three more options" → concept-sheet.
