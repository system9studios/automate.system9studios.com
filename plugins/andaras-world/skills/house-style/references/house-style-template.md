# House style template

```markdown
# Andara — Concept Art House Style
**Status:** draft
**Version:** HS v0

## Purpose
Concept exploration and reference for design decisions and, later, the client's art pipeline. Not final assets.

## Rendering
- Approach: <e.g. loose painterly with visible brushwork / clean line + flat color / value-first grayscale then color>
- Finish level: <thumbnail-rough / mid / polished>, per stage (exploration vs. selected refinement)
- Edges: <hard where form turns, soft in atmosphere…>

## Value and color
- Value structure: <e.g. 3–4 value groups, clear silhouette against background>
- Palette strategy: <e.g. desaturated base, one accent per image>
- World color logic: <how the world's distinctive terrain and phenomena read in color> [needs world-builder input]

## Line
- <none / structural only / inked contour, weight variation>

## Light
- Default: <e.g. overcast frontier daylight, single warm key>
- Exceptions: interiors, supernatural effects, night scenes

## Framing by subject type
| Type | View | Aspect | Background |
|---|---|---|---|
| character / species | full-body, three-quarter, neutral pose | 2:3 | flat or minimal |
| creature | three-quarter + scale figure | 3:2 | minimal |
| item / vehicle | orthographic or three-quarter prop sheet | 1:1 | flat |
| location / environment | establishing shot, human-scale figure for scale | 3:2 | full |

## How the world shows
- Era layers: <how each era of construction canon names reads differently>
- Salvage vs. manufactured: <visual tells>
- Ways of acting on the world (as canon names them): <visual language for each, as the tone guardrails frame them>
- Mundane first: extraordinary elements are rare and small in frame unless they're the subject

## Exclusions
- No text, lettering, logos, watermarks, UI
- No named-artist, studio, or franchise looks
- No post-apocalyptic ruin clichés (the world is lived-in)

## Changelog
- HS v0 — <date> — initial draft
```
