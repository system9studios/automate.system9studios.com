# Examples

**Prompt:** `/concept-study CA-001 A`
**Behavior:** Loads concept-study. CA-001 A is selected. Must-keeps: overpass-concrete mass as the gate's spine; salvage-plate repairs at head height; the thin, patched wall line to either side. Free to change: gate hardware, figures, weather. Suggests `value-thumbs`, `paintover`, `redesign`. Writes `ST-CA-001-1` on the sheet and hands off to Creative Coach, or prints the brief if no Creative Coach agent is loaded. Next action: "Take ST-CA-001-1 to Creative Coach."

**Prompt:** "Here's my Krita version of the gate." (attaches `CA-001-S1.kra` export)
**Behavior:** Records it under *Studies*. Asks whether they want a design-fit check or to promote it. Doesn't comment on brushwork or technique, and points them to Creative Coach for that.

**Prompt:** "Promote it and select it."
**Behavior:** Adds candidate `S1`, marks it `Selected — visual reference`, marks A `Superseded`, reminds them to upload `CA-001-S1.png` to the ChatGPT project files.

**Non-triggers:** "How do I get cleaner edges in Krita?" → creative-coach. "Concept the inn" → concept-sheet.
