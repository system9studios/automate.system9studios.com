# Canon proposal template

Modeled on the approved cosmology page. Drop sections that don't apply; keep the order.

```markdown
**Status:** proposed
**Resolves tracker items:** §<n> <section>: <items>
**Depends on:** <approved pages>
**Decided by the owner (this session):** <bullets, one per decision>
---
## Summary
<3–6 sentences. The mechanism or idea, then why it matters for play.>
---
## 1. <Core topic>

### How it works (rules, costs, limits)

<What this gives the game: mechanics, content hooks, regional variety.>

---
## Tracker impact
**Resolved:** …
**Partially addressed:** …
**New tracker items:** …
```

Rules:
- Bullets for rules, prose for summary. Tables where the approved canon pages use them.
- "Why this works" subsections are welcome: they record the owner's rationale for later sessions.
- No player-facing prose. Example sayings, names, or objects are allowed as `[PROPOSED]` design examples.
