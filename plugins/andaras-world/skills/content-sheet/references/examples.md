# Examples

**Prompt:** "Design the militia commander."
**Behavior:** Loads content-sheet (Named NPC). Checks prerequisites: the village's identity and "why hasn't it died" answer aren't approved yet, so asks whether to proceed on `[PROPOSED]` assumptions. On yes, drafts `npc-militia-commander` with name `(placeholder)`, Desire/Fear/Secret each as 2–3 options, Connections to the militia faction and perimeter zone (both listed under *Missing sheets*), writing briefs for greeting and long description, and runtime notes (entity template + behavior binding; "reputation-gated dialogue" listed as a language gap). Reports coverage: 1/12–18 named NPCs.

**Prompt:** "Where are we against the content targets?"
**Behavior:** Counts sheets by type and status, reports the §18 table with approved/draft split and the next gap in §19 phase order.

**Non-triggers:** "Decide the neighboring cities" → canon-proposal. "Write the inn's description" → the owner writes; line-edit reviews. "Convert the commons sheets to .aw" → aw-author.
