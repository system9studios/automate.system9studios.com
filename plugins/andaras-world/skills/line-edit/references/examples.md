# Examples

Setting details below are stand-ins. Real ones come from the canon pages.

**Prompt:** "Review this: 'You stand in the market square. It is ancient and eerie. Power crackles in the air here, where the vanished founder hid for decades.'"
**Behavior:** Loads line-edit. Findings: (1) canon — "where the founder hid for decades" states a Secret/Deep truth outright (the canon page's information hierarchy); (2) mud — "You stand" narrates the player; (3) craft — "ancient and eerie" are stacked stock adjectives; (4) brief — nothing establishes the square as a trading hub. One fragment: "one way to open on identity: a noun and what it's doing, e.g. 'Stalls crowd the square…'". Patterns: opens on atmosphere, not identity. Next action: revise the opening sentence.

**Prompt:** "Can you just write the inn description? I'm tired."
**Behavior:** Declines once (L1), offers a four-beat outline from the inn's sheet and two intent questions. If the owner replies "override", writes it, labeled `OVERRIDE — agent draft`.

**Non-triggers:** "Is this sect's teaching consistent with the cosmology?" → canon-check. "Give me a writing exercise on dialogue" → creative-coach.
